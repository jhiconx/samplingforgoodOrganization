Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current Sampling For Good Distribution request form. It is not a reproduction of the iconx.io corporate Privacy Policy. The corporate policy and this notice should be reviewed before broader public launch.

What information you submit

When you request samples, you may provide your phone number, email address, selected brands, desired quantity, preferred shipping date, charity choice, and any notes you type or dictate. At least one contact method is required to submit a request.

How the information is used

We use your information to receive, review, and respond to sample-distribution requests from organizations, including fraternities and sororities. Submissions are emailed to the Sampling For Good team at samples@iconx.io. A request does not guarantee fulfillment, product availability, a donation, or a brand partnership.

Service providers

The request website uses Vercel for hosting and server-side processing and Resend to route request emails. Technical information such as IP addresses may be processed for security and spam prevention. Do not include sensitive personal information in free-text notes.

Brand connections

Brand partner connections are not yet active in this request form. If direct brand fulfillment is added in the future, this notice should be updated to describe any information shared for that purpose.

Requests and questions

For questions about information submitted through this form or to request correction or deletion, contact samples@iconx.io.
